### CLIM-000069: Independent measurement of global OH variability
- **Subfield:** Methane and terrestrial nitrous oxide
- **Open:** Constrain methane-weighted hydroxyl abundance independently of methane observations used to infer emissions. Separate OH variability from tracer-emission and transport uncertainty.
- **Status:** A decades-old attribution obstacle; the 2022 analysis of 2020 illustrates material sink effects on inferred source growth.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Makes methane verification robust to changing atmospheric cleansing.
- **Key works:** Peng et al. (2022), [“Wetland emission and atmospheric sink changes explain methane growth in 2020”](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05447-w).
- **Related:** Radical metrology; jointly identifiable chemical inversions.
